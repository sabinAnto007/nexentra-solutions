One record, from booking to settlement

A freight/trip carries its status through a strict lifecycle — nothing skips a stage, and every stage is visible to whoever needs it.

1

Booked

Party, route, cargo and financials entered. Freight cost, GST and party advance captured up front.

2

Dispatch

Driver and vehicle assigned — auto-resolved from the driver's current vehicle, with an audit flag if a dispatcher overrides it.

3

In Transit

Trip expenses logged against 7 categories — diesel, FastTag, driver advance, tolls, loading, maintenance, misc.

4

Delivered

Trip marked delivered against the expected delivery date, closing the operational side of the record.

5

Completed

Driver settlement status tracked through to Paid — Pending, Partially Paid, Paid. The record is now financially closed.

Everything that ships today

Grouped the way the product is actually organised — no roadmap items, no "coming soon."

Fleet Master

  • Full vehicle record — type, capacity, odometer, service schedule
  • Versioned compliance documents (FC, insurance, permit, PUC)
  • Status & availability tracked separately (Active vs. On Trip)

Driver Master

  • Licence, Aadhaar and photo, versioned the same way as vehicles
  • Full vehicle assignment history — never a hard delete
  • Employment type, salary, emergency contact on record

Freight / Trips

  • Draft → Booked → In Transit → Delivered → Completed
  • 7-category trip expense ledger, driver settlement tracking
  • Auto-resolved driver/vehicle pairing, with an override audit flag

Reports & Exports

  • Dedicated report screens for Vehicle, Driver, Party, Freight
  • Excel & CSV export on every report
  • Scheduled recurring email delivery — daily, weekly, monthly

Reminders & Alerts

  • Configurable thresholds — 30/15/7/1/0 days before expiry
  • In-app notification centre with unread state
  • Daily background sweep, de-duplicated alerts

Multi-Branch & Roles

  • Every record scoped to a branch, org-wide reporting on top
  • Three-tier roles — Owner, Branch Manager, Staff
  • Configurable auto-numbering per entity, org-wide
